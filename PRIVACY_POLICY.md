# Privacy Policy for Coloring Creatures

**Last Updated: November 15, 2024**

## Introduction

Coloring Creatures ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("App") handles information.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

Coloring Creatures is designed with privacy as a priority. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including but not limited to:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Crash reports
- Any other personally identifiable information

## Data Storage

All data created within the App is stored locally on your device only:

- **Colored Images**: Saved creations are stored in your device's local storage
- **App Preferences**: Settings like music on/off are stored locally
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not sync any data to our servers or third-party services

## Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

### Photo Library Access (iOS)
- **Purpose**: To save your colored animal creations to your device's photo library
- **Scope**: Write-only access to save images
- **Usage**: Only activated when you tap "Save to Pen"
- **Optional**: You can deny this permission and still use the app; images will save to app storage only

### Storage Access (Android)
- **Purpose**: To save colored images to device storage
- **Usage**: Only for saving your creations locally

### Audio (Background Music)
- **Purpose**: To play optional background music
- **Usage**: Can be toggled on/off in the app
- **No Recording**: We do not record or transmit any audio

## Third-Party Services

Coloring Creatures does NOT use any third-party services, including:

- No analytics platforms
- No advertising networks
- No social media integrations
- No crash reporting services
- No cloud storage services

## Children's Privacy

Coloring Creatures is designed for children aged 4 and up. We take children's privacy seriously:

- We do not collect any personal information from children
- We do not allow children to share personal information
- We do not use targeted advertising
- The app works completely offline
- No account creation or login required

This app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## Data Security

Since we do not collect any data:

- There is no data to be breached
- There is no data to be sold or shared
- There is no data to be lost

All creations are stored on your device and are as secure as your device itself.

## In-App Purchases

Currently, Coloring Creatures does NOT include in-app purchases. If this changes in the future:

- We will update this Privacy Policy
- Purchase information will be handled by Apple/Google
- We will not receive or store payment information

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- For significant changes, displaying a notice in the app

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

- Email: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com
- GitHub: https://github.com/bolkE12/ColoringApp

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

- **Access**: Since we don't collect data, there is no data to access
- **Deletion**: You can delete the app to remove all locally stored data
- **Portability**: All your creations are stored as standard PNG files on your device
- **Objection**: You can deny photo library permission at any time in device settings

## Compliance

This app complies with:

- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) - through non-collection of data
- CalOPPA (California Online Privacy Protection Act)

## Summary

**In short**: Coloring Creatures respects your privacy by not collecting any information at all. Everything happens on your device, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
