## Support Response Templates

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I save my colored animals?**
A: Tap the "Save to Pen" button when you're done coloring. Your creation will be saved to the Animal Pen where you can view all your masterpieces!

**Q: Can I change the animal's name?**
A: Yes! Tap the left/right arrows next to the name to cycle through different silly names. When you find one you like, tap "Save to Pen" to keep it.

**Q: How many animals can I create?**
A: Unlimited! You can create as many hybrid animals as you want and save them all to your Animal Pen.

**Q: Why does the app need photo library access?**
A: We only request photo library access to save your colored creations. The images are saved locally on your device and never shared or uploaded anywhere.

**Q: Does this app collect my child's data?**
A: No! We don't collect any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

**Q: Can I use this app offline?**
A: Yes! Creature Coloring works completely offline. Perfect for car rides, planes, or anywhere without internet.

**Q: What age is this app appropriate for?**
A: Creature Coloring is rated 4+ and is perfect for preschool through elementary school children. The simple interface makes it easy for even young children to use independently.
