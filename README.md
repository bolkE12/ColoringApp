## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I unlock all animals?
Tap the "Unlock All Animals" button on any screen. You can unlock the entire collection for just $0.99!

### How do I save my creations?
After coloring your creature, tap the "Save to Pen" button. Your creation will be saved to your Animal Pen.

### How do I share my creations?
Open your Animal Pen, tap on any saved creature, and use the download button to save it to your iPad's photo library. You can then share it from the Photos app.

### The app isn't working properly
Try these steps:
1. Close the app completely and reopen it
2. Restart your iPad
3. Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store

### Privacy & Safety
Coloring Creatures is designed for children ages 4+. We do not collect any personal data, and there are no ads or third-party tracking.
